Quiet Arrows <!-- TODO(John): App Store name --> (QuietArrows on your home screen) is a portrait puzzle game made by Shenaniganeers ("we", "us", or "our"). Bent arrows lie on the faces of a translucent solid; tap one and it leaves if its path is clear. We built this game to collect as little as possible about you. This page explains exactly what that means.

## What We Collect

We collect nothing ourselves. Quiet Arrows has no accounts, no login, and no sign-up. We do not collect your name, email, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or health data, and we run no analytics or tracking service of our own. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and the game never asks for permission to. The app does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency and never reads your device's advertising identifier.

## Advertising

Unless you buy Remove Ads, Quiet Arrows shows one small banner ad, supplied by Google AdMob. We ask Google for **non-personalized ads only**: ads chosen from the context of the app and your general location, not from a profile of you.

Where Google requires it, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and ads are shown only as your choice allows. When Google requires an entry point, Settings shows **Ad privacy choices** so you can review or change your choice at any time. Changing your choices stops the banner.

To serve and measure the banner, Google's software receives:
- Your IP address, used to estimate a general location
- A device identifier
- Which ads were shown and tapped, and how you interacted with them
- Crash and performance data

Google's use of this information is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Our Services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Game Center (Optional)

Game Center is **off by default**, and nothing signs in when the app launches. Settings has two separate switches. **Participate** turns on Game Center achievements. **Share Times** lets your ranked and weekly puzzle times be posted to Game Center leaderboards, and it only has an effect when Participate is also on. When both are on, your Game Center player, your achievements, and your times appear on public leaderboards run by Apple Game Center. Turning either switch off stops further sharing.

## What Stays on Your Device

The game saves your progress locally so you can pick up where you left off: your level progress and current collection and level, your best times and stars, your settings (arrow width and appearance/theme), your Game Center switches, and which one-time purchases you own. All of this is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

## In-App Purchases

Quiet Arrows offers three optional one-time purchases that you can restore: Remove Ads, Slate Theme, and Ember Theme. The themes change appearance only; Light and Dark are free. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Apple processes the transaction and tells the app only whether you own a given product. Restore Purchases is in Settings.
