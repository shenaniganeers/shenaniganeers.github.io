# 3D Arrow Puzzle, Quietly Support

**Last updated: November 20, 2026**

3D Arrow Puzzle, Quietly (QuietArrows on your home screen) is made by Shenaniganeers. This page answers the common questions. If yours is not here, write to us at the address at the bottom.

## How to Play

Bent arrows lie on the faces of a translucent solid. Drag to turn the solid and look around. An arrow leads with its head: tap one and it slides away the way it points, if its path is clear. Orange wrap edges carry an arrow onto the next face. A blocked arrow turns red, counts as a crash, and stays put until its path is free. Clear every arrow to finish; finish with no crashes for three stars. Weekly holds this week's puzzles, and the Library holds every pack. **How to play** in Settings replays the short guide at any time.

## Hints and Centering

**Hint** highlights an arrow you can free right now (turning the solid to it if needed) and adds a small time penalty, but never costs stars. **Center** turns the solid so a face or corner squarely faces you; tap it again to step to the next one.

## Purchases

- **Remove Ads** removes the banner ad. **Slate** and **Ember** are optional themes; Light and Dark are free.
- All purchases are one-time and handled by Apple. To get them back on a new device or after reinstalling, open **Settings** and tap **Restore Purchases**, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought them with.
- For refunds, Apple handles all payments, so request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Game Center

Game Center is off by default. Turn on **Game Center** in Settings for achievements, and **Share my times** to post your ranked and weekly times to the leaderboards. You can view leaderboards without sharing.

## Your Progress

Progress, best times, and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account, so deleting the app deletes your progress; purchases can always be restored.
